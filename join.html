Who it's for

If you're young and you want to help, that's the whole requirement

Rotaract is for young adults. Beyond that we're not fussy — students, people in their first job, self-employed, between jobs, new to Honiara, born here. All of it works. Confirm the club's actual age range before publishing

  • Aged roughly [18–30] Placeholder
  • Living in or around Honiara
  • Able to give a few hours a month — more if you want to, less if life's busy
  • Happy to muck in: no one's above carrying chairs
How it works

Four steps, none of them scary

  1. 01

    Get in touch or just turn up

    Message us on Instagram or fill in the form below. We'll tell you when and where the next meeting is. Placeholder

  2. 02

    Come to a couple of meetings

    Visit as many times as you like before deciding. There's no cost and no obligation to join. Placeholder

  3. 03

    Fill in a membership form

    A short form and a membership fee of [amount] a [period], which covers club running costs. Confirm the real fee

  4. 04

    Get inducted and get stuck in

    A short welcome at a meeting, then pick a project or committee that interests you. Placeholder

What membership looks like

What you'd actually get out of it

Being straight with you: it takes real time, and nobody gets paid. Here's what you get in return. Placeholder

  • Real experience running projects, budgets and teams
  • Public speaking and leadership training through Rotary
  • A network across Honiara — and access to Rotaract clubs worldwide
  • Genuine friendships outside your usual circle
  • The occasional very good night out

Questions

Things people usually ask

Do I have to be invited?
No. Anyone can get in touch and come along to a meeting. Placeholder
How much time does it take?
Roughly [X hours a month] for meetings, plus whatever you choose to give to projects. Members with busy periods step back and step up again later — that's normal. Placeholder
Is there a fee?
Yes — [amount] per [period], which covers club administration and Rotaract district dues. If cost is a barrier, talk to us; we'd rather have you in the club. Confirm the real fee
Do I need to be connected to Rotary already?
Not at all. Most of our members had no Rotary connection before joining. Placeholder
What if I'm too old for Rotaract?
You're still very welcome as a volunteer, a partner or a supporter — and our sponsoring Rotary club would love to hear from you. Get in touch. Placeholder
Can I volunteer without becoming a member?
Absolutely. Plenty of people help out on project days without joining the club. Let us know and we'll add you to the call-out list. Placeholder
