About the club

We're young Solomon Islanders who decided to get on with it

Rotaract Honiara brings together young people who want to do something useful with their time — and to grow while they're at it. Here's who we are and where we came from.

What is Rotaract Club of Honiara?

We're part of Rotaract — a worldwide network of clubs run by and for young people. Ours is based right here in Honiara.

Our members come from all over: students, first-jobbers, small business owners, people working in the public service. What everyone shares is a willingness to give up a few evenings and weekends for something bigger than themselves.

  • Open to young people across Honiara — no particular background required
  • Member-led: the projects we run are the ones members bring forward
  • Backed by Rotary International's global network and standards

What we stand for

Five things we hold ourselves to

These aren't posters on a wall — they're how we decide what to take on and how we run it.

01

Service above self

The Rotary motto, and the reason the club exists. We put the community's needs ahead of our own convenience.

02

Young people leading

Members run the projects, hold the budgets and make the calls. Mentors advise; they don't take over.

03

Doing what we said we'd do

We'd rather promise less and deliver it than announce something big and quietly drop it.

04

Open about our money

Funds raised are accounted for and reported back to the people who gave them.

05

Everyone's welcome

Whatever your background, church, island or job — if you want to help, there's a place for you here.

Part of something bigger

Our link to Rotary International

Rotaract is Rotary International's programme for young people. Clubs like ours exist in almost every country in the world, and we're sponsored and supported by our local Rotary club.

In practice that means we're plugged into a global network of training, grants and people who've run projects like ours before — while staying entirely locally led.

  • Sponsored by a local Rotary club
  • Part of Rotary District 9910
  • Access to Rotary Foundation grants for larger community projects
