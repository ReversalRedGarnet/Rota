Support us

Want to donate or sponsor something?

We don't collect payments through this website. Get in touch and we'll tell you exactly what's needed right now and where your support would go.

💰 Money

Fund a project or sponsor an event. Email us and we'll send bank details and confirm what the money is for, in writing.

📦 Goods & materials

Building materials, school supplies, printing, food, transport, venue hire — often more useful to us than cash.

🤝 Time & skills

Join a project day, run a training session for our members, or mentor someone through a project.

Every donation gets a written acknowledgement and, once the project is done, a short report showing what it paid for.

Before you write

Quick answers

How long until someone replies?
Usually within a week. Instagram DMs tend to get answered fastest.
Can I visit a meeting without joining?
Yes, and there's no cost. Message us first so we can tell you where to go.
Can my school or organisation ask the club for help?
Please do get in touch. We can't take on everything, but we'd rather hear about it and be honest about what we can manage.
Can I use your photos or logo?
Ask us first. The Rotaract name and emblem have usage rules set by Rotary International.
