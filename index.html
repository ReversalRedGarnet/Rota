What we do

Three things, done properly

Everything the club does falls into one of these. No mission-statement waffle.

Community Service

Hands-on projects around Honiara — clean-ups, school support, health awareness, whatever the community actually asks for. Placeholder

Leadership & Skills

Workshops, public speaking, running real budgets and real teams. The stuff that looks good on a CV because you actually did it. Placeholder

Friendship & Fun

Socials, sports, our annual ball — the club is a genuinely good way to meet people outside your usual circle. Placeholder

Our approach

How we make change that lasts

Anyone can run a one-off event. Here's how we try to make sure it actually sticks.

  1. 01

    Listen first

    We start by asking the community what they need, not by deciding for them. Every project begins with conversations. Placeholder

  2. 02

    Partner up

    We work alongside local organisations, schools and businesses who know the ground — and we're upfront about who did what. Placeholder

  3. 03

    Show up and do the work

    Members give their own time and hands. Nothing gets outsourced to a photo opportunity. Placeholder

  4. 04

    Report back honestly

    What worked, what didn't, where the money went. Our partners and supporters get the full picture, not just the highlights. Placeholder
