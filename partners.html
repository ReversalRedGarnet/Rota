Why work with us

What a partner actually gets

We're a small club, so we're straightforward to deal with — one point of contact, a clear plan, and a written report at the end. No layers of approval. Placeholder

  • Your support goes directly into a named project, not general overheads
  • Photos, figures and a written report you can use in your own reporting
  • Visibility with a young audience across our socials and at events
  • A team of volunteers who turn up when they say they will
  • The credibility of Rotary International's global standards behind us

Ways to help

Support doesn't have to mean money

Some of the most useful help we've had cost nothing at all.

Fund a project

Financial support

Back a specific project, or sponsor one of our fundraising events. We'll agree what the money covers up front and report back on it afterwards.

  • Named as a project supporter
  • Written report with photos and figures
  • Recognition at the event and on our socials

Give goods or space

In-kind support

Materials, printing, food, transport, or a room to meet in. Genuinely as valuable to us as cash, and often easier for a business to give.

  • Named as a project supporter
  • Recognition on our socials
  • An open invitation to see the work

Lend your people or skills

Skills & volunteering

Send a team to a project day, run a training session for our members, or mentor a member through a project. Great for staff development, too.

  • A team volunteering day with your staff
  • Photos you can share with your own audience
  • A direct line into Honiara's young workforce
