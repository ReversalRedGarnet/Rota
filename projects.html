Where the money and the hours go

If you're considering funding or partnering with us, this is the part that matters.

  1. 01

    Every project has a named lead

    One member owns each project end to end, and reports to the board on it. Placeholder

  2. 02

    Budgets are approved before we start

    Nothing gets spent without the club agreeing to it first. Placeholder

  3. 03

    Sponsors get a written report

    Photos, receipts and outcomes — sent after the project, not just asked for at the start. Placeholder
